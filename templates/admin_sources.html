Essential Distinction: Network Reachability ≠ Content Verification

A 200 OK response from an official website only proves network reachability. It never verifies legal accuracy. Official government gazettes, notifications, or statutory portals frequently change URL paths or restructure sub-pages. Legal content is verified exclusively when an authorized evaluator inspects the actual statutory text against the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
