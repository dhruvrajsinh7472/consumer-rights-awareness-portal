Community Engagement Project (CEP)

Technology for Consumer Rights Awareness

A student-led civic technology initiative bridging statutory consumer protection laws in India with accessible digital navigation tools for everyday citizens.

Academic Civic Tech Open Source & Self-Contained

1. Community Problem & Project Motivation

Despite the enactment of the progressive Consumer Protection Act, 2019, millions of Indian consumers remain unaware of their statutory remedies when confronted with e-commerce refund denials, defective goods, unfair contract terms, or deceptive marketing claims.

Surveys reveal that most citizens wrongly believe filing a dispute requires hiring an advocate, paying substantial legal retainers, or physically traveling to court buildings. Additionally, confusion between the National Consumer Helpline (NCH 1915) for pre-litigation and the e-Daakhil digital court filing system prevents timely redressal within the statutory two-year limitation period.

2. Core Academic Objectives

1
Decode Statutory Consumer Rights: Translate complex legislative provisions under Section 2(9) of CPA 2019 into practical, plain-language guidance accessible to non-lawyers.
2
Document Repeatable Resolution Workflows: Provide step-by-step resolution roadmaps and required evidence checklists for frequent real-world issues (e-commerce, warranty, MRP overcharging, UPI debits).
3
Direct Citizens to Verified Official Channels: Prevent fraudulent intermediary services by maintaining a direct directory of legitimate government portals (.gov.in / .nic.in).
4
Promote Accessible, Self-Sufficient Civic Tech: Build using lightweight, privacy-respecting technology (Python/Flask, SQLite, Semantic HTML) that requires zero paid cloud lock-ins or third-party user tracking.

3. Research & Architectural Methodology

  • Traceability: Every legal right, guide, and FAQ is mapped directly to a statutory source record in our relational SQLite database.
  • Idempotent Versioning: Core datasets are version-controlled in structured JSON format, enabling continuous collaborative review.
  • Zero Commercial Incentives: No sponsored links, advertisements, monetization, or vendor partnerships exist on this platform.

Academic Legal Disclaimer

This portal is an educational and public-awareness tool created strictly for academic and community engagement purposes. The information provided does NOT constitute legal advice or formal representation. Consumers seeking judicial redressal must consult official government portals (such as consumerhelpline.gov.in or edaakhil.nic.in) or seek independent professional counsel.
