Facing a marketplace dispute? Follow the 4-step framework.

Most consumer grievances in India can be resolved without costly court litigation when you follow documented escalation procedures and gather the correct evidence upfront.

01
Identify Violation
Pinpoint the exact right violated under CPA 2019.
02
Collect Evidence

03
