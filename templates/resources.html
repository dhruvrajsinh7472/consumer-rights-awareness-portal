Official Navigation & Non-Intermediary Notice

This educational portal does NOT act as an API, intermediary agent, or direct filing submission channel for government authorities. All formal consumer grievances and commission filings must be submitted directly by citizens using the authentic government web links listed below.

URL Verification Tip: Always verify that official Indian government portals end with .gov.in or .nic.in to guard against phishing and duplicate private intermediary platforms.

Crucial Distinction: National Consumer Helpline (NCH) vs. Consumer Court (e-Jagriti)

Understanding when to use pre-litigation grievance assistance versus formal legal court proceedings:

Pre-Litigation Alternative NCH (1915)

National Consumer Helpline

  • Purpose: Alternate dispute resolution and informal company mediation via corporate convergence.
  • Procedure: Call 1915 or register ticket on INGRAM portal. Completely free; no evidence affidavits required.
  • Timeline: Typically processed within 15–30 days.
  • Scope Limits: Does NOT issue legally binding judgments or order punitive damages. Relies on voluntary corporate settlement.
Quasi-Judicial Court e-Jagriti Commissions

Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions

  • Purpose: Formal judicial litigation before District, State, or National Commissions under CPA 2019.
  • Procedure: File petition online via e-Jagriti (e-jagriti.gov.in) with verified complaint and evidence index. In-person representation permitted.
  • Filing Fees: NIL fee for claims up to ₹5 Lakh; tiered nominal fees up to ₹1,000 for ₹50 Lakh.
  • Scope Limits: Issues legally binding judgments, execution decrees, and enforceable compensation orders.

Verified Government Services & Portals

National Consumer Helpline (NCH / INGRAM)

VERIFIED: 2026-10-25
Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India
NCH_PORTAL
What This Service Handles:

Pre-litigation consumer complaints against over 800 registered convergence partner companies, e-commerce refund delays, telecom billing errors, and consumer rights awareness guidance.

What This Service Does NOT Do:

Does NOT act as a court; cannot issue binding injunctions, cannot penalize companies, and cannot award monetary compensation. If a company rejects convergence mediation, the dispute must be escalated to a Consumer Commission.

Verified Contact Channels: Toll-Free 1915 (8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, all days except national holidays) • WhatsApp: +91 8800001915 • SMS: 8800001915 • NCH App & UMANG App.
Domain: consumerhelpline.gov.in Visit Official NCH Portal

e-Jagriti Unified Consumer Court Portal

VERIFIED: 2026-10-25
Department of Consumer Affairs & National Informatics Centre (NIC)
E_JAGRITI
What This Service Handles:

Unified digital case filing, electronic fee payment, evidence document uploads, daily cause list tracking, case order access, and virtual hearing video-conferencing across District (DCDRC), State (SCDRC), and National (NCDRC) Commissions.

What This Service Does NOT Do:

Does NOT provide informal customer support chat or instant refunds. Complaints submitted here initiate formal judicial proceedings requiring structured petitions and evidentiary verification.

Appropriate Use Cases: Filing formal legal complaints against defective products, service deficiencies, or product liability injuries when informal pre-litigation resolution fails.
Domain: e-jagriti.gov.in Visit e-Jagriti Portal

Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) & BIS Care

VERIFIED: 2026-10-25
Bureau of Indian Standards, Government of India
BIS_PORTAL
What This Service Handles:

Verification of ISI marks, electronics CRS R-numbers, 6-digit Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) on gold jewellery, and citizen grievance registration against counterfeit or sub-standard certified goods.

What This Service Does NOT Do:

Does NOT resolve financial refund disputes or delivery delays between individual consumers and online retailers.

Official Verification Tools: BIS Care Mobile App (Google Play Store & Apple App Store) • Manakonline Portal (manakonline.in) for online licence checks.
Domain: bis.gov.in Visit Official BIS Website

Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)

VERIFIED: 2026-10-25
Statutory Authority under Section 10 of Consumer Protection Act, 2019
CCPA_DARK_PATTERNS_2023
What This Service Handles:

Class-action investigations into unfair trade practices affecting consumers as a class, mandatory recall of dangerous goods, penalizing misleading advertisements, and enforcement of the 2023 Dark Patterns Guidelines.

What This Service Does NOT Do:

Does NOT adjudicate individual civil monetary compensation claims. Acts as an executive regulatory watchdog for consumer rights enforcement.

Email: cp-da@nic.in Visit Official CCPA Page

Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman (RBI CMS)

VERIFIED: 2026-10-25
Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
RBI_TAT_CIRCULAR_2019
What This Service Handles:

Grievance redressal against banks, NBFCs, and payment system operators for failed UPI transactions, unauthorized electronic debits, and ATM cash non-dispense where the bank failed to resolve within 30 days.

What This Service Does NOT Do:

Does NOT handle commercial disputes regarding product quality of goods bought with the card/UPI.

Verified Contact: Portal: cms.rbi.org.in • Toll-Free Helpline: 14448 (operational 9:30 AM to 5:15 PM).
Domain: cms.rbi.org.in Visit RBI CMS Portal
