Our Non-Negotiable Provenance Standards

1. Primary Source Mandate Only primary gazettes, official ministries (DoCA, BIS, RBI, CCPA), and statutory texts serve as legal authority. Academic research papers inform usability only.
2. Explicit Verification States Distinguishes reachable links from verified content. Unverified or pending claims are transparently flagged or withheld from publication.
3. Zero AI Hallucination AI assistance is confined to code architecture and formatting. Every statutory fee, limitation period, and jurisdictional threshold is verified by human research.
