Public Ethics & Transparency

Privacy and Data Handling Policy

Our commitment to public privacy, data minimization, and ethical educational technology.

1. Zero Tracking and No Commercial Cookies

As an educational Community Engagement Project, our architecture strictly respects visitor privacy:

  • We do NOT use tracking cookies, commercial analytics scripts, or behavioral pixel trackers.
  • We do NOT require account registration, phone numbers, or email addresses to access guides, rights, or quiz assessments.
  • All search queries performed through the live search bar are processed locally on this server without transmitting terms to third-party advertising brokers.

2. Anonymous Feedback Collection

When you submit comments or corrections through our Feedback Form, we only store:

  • The chosen category (e.g., Content Correction, Usability Suggestion).
  • The text message you write.
  • The optional 1–5 rating.
  • A timestamp of submission.

We do NOT record IP addresses, user agents, or personal identifiers in the feedback database table.

3. External Government Portals

This website provides navigational outbound links to official Indian government dispute websites (such as consumerhelpline.gov.in and edaakhil.nic.in). Once you navigate away from our educational portal, the respective privacy and term policies of those government agencies govern your interactions.

Questions Regarding Academic Privacy Practices

For inquiries regarding this Community Engagement Project or data ethics practices, submit a note through our Feedback page.
